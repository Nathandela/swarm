01 · Diagnosis

Aligned in color. Under-expressed in behavior.

The shipped design has strong fundamentals: a dark-only Slate ladder, careful status semantics, honest safety copy, pinned chat structure, and disciplined component states. What it lacks is a signature composition. Most screens could still belong to any thoughtful developer tool.

KEEP / 01

Safety is legible

Needs-you, working, review, completed, and error already have distinct semantic colors and words. Preserve that exact mapping.

KEEP / 02

Chat has the right frame

Fixed header, one scrolling transcript, pinned composer. This is the right information architecture; the redesign should strengthen it.

OPEN / 03

Identity stops at the palette

The atmospheric swarm exists in repository art and the launcher, but not in the app’s spatial grammar, transitions, or empty states.

OPEN / 04

Everything has equal gravity

Rows, sections, system warnings, and key actions often use similar boxes. The user’s eye receives structure, but not a compelling field of priority.

02 · Three directions

Three ways to bring the Night Orchestra inside.

All three keep the app implementable with native Android views and the existing tokens. They differ in how much atmosphere they spend and where they place it. Every frame in the workshop can be switched among all three.

04 · Identity grammar

The swarm motif must do a job.

The interface should never paste a decorative S behind content. Each visual device earns its place by carrying product meaning. This is how the atmospheric identity becomes a system rather than wallpaper.

Trajectory

Connects a session’s past, present, and next action. Used in state transitions and grouped timeline edges.

means continuity · never network topology

Beacon

The single authoritative status mark for an agent, machine, or operation. Word and color always travel together.

means current state · never decoration

Atmospheric layer

Separates hierarchy at destination headers, onboarding, and empty states without adding another bordered card.

means depth · never a content background

Field

Shows several actors belonging to one machine or one workflow. Best for scope selection and connection health.

means coordinated plurality · never a hive

05 · Implementation path

Make Signal Field the base; borrow Atlas sparingly.

The strongest route is a hybrid with a clear hierarchy: Signal Field for everyday interaction, Night Atlas for first-run and empty/system moments, and Constellation only where the product truly shows several actors at once.

PHASE 01 · FOUNDATION

Give the kit a signature

High impact, low product risk. No navigation or protocol changes.

  • Add trail, beacon, atmospheric header, and field primitives
  • Refine nav/title rhythm and bottom tab treatment
  • Move welcome, empty, and offline states onto the new grammar
  • Keep the canonical token and contrast pipeline untouched
PHASE 02 · CORE FLOW

Redesign daily use

Inbox, conversation, decision, and launch—where identity earns repeat value.

  • Session timeline rails and stronger state grouping
  • Conversation continuity trail and quieter tool rows
  • Inline decision card resolution motion
  • Preset-led launch with machine context visible first
PHASE 03 · SYSTEM

Complete the world

Utility surfaces and edge cases inherit the same visual language.

  • Connection field and machine health detail
  • Activity trajectory and file/output presentation
  • Push, toast, stale, loading, and repair choreography
  • Tablet, font-scale, TalkBack, and reduced-motion passes

Keep

  • Short, user-side copy and honest state words
  • One luminous accent per statement
  • Large quiet fields around important decisions
  • Native Android semantics and 48 dp targets

Avoid

  • Full scenic illustrations behind active content
  • Lines that suggest false data relationships
  • Continuous neon, particles, or decorative looping
  • Changing semantic colors to “make a screen prettier”
