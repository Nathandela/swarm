Visual thesis

Orchestration, not a hive.

“Swarm” can become noisy, insect-like, or diagrammatic very quickly. This direction makes it spacious instead: autonomous lights cross a living landscape while a quiet piece of infrastructure keeps the system coherent.

Guardrails

Keep the world calm.

The identity works when the system feels capable, legible, and quietly alive. More objects and more glow would reduce that confidence.

Keep

  • One enormous celestial or environmental anchor.
  • Five to seven broad atmospheric layers.
  • Small signals using the shipped semantic colors.
  • Matte grain, smooth edges, and generous negative space.

Avoid

  • Literal bees, honeycomb motifs, and insect imagery.
  • Neon cyberpunk density or generic “AI network” nodes.
  • A different bright color for every agent.
  • Putting UI screenshots inside the painted world.
