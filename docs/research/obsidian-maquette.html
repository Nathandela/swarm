swarm — Obsidian maquette

Normative. The :root block in this file carries the 35 tokens tokens.json transcribes (phase O2); every frame, control and mark below is drawn at those values, on the PB-DS-1 spacing scale. Layout, flows and status taxonomy are unchanged from the shipped app — only the skin moves. The specular sweep loops here for display; in production it fires once, on promotion to NeedsInput.

The mark

Solid Wedge, re-rendered

Geometry untouched — the wedge and its cursor block are the identity. Only the material moves: champagne on warm obsidian, the same key-light as every card. The themed (monochrome) variant satisfies Android 13+ themed icons.

The emotional core

Where attention lives: scanning the inbox, deciding on the sheet. The lit slab carries the stronger key-light (--p-lit-fx) and the one sweep; the sheet is the only vertical gradient in the app — the heaviest material, reserved for the moment of decision. The sheet's headline is the blocking question itself; the literal command stays in the well.
