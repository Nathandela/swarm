## Research informing the plan

Human conversational gap distributions vary with context. They inform the shape of the latency measurement; agent tool latency makes a human median a poor release threshold. Floor-taking differs from interrupting, backchanneling and overhearing. Score a system that stays silent on every example for missed turns as well as false entries. Voice Activity Projection provides a two-channel hold/shift framework. Its two-channel setup cannot establish multi-party addressing. [Cross-language turn timing](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.0903616106) · [Voice Activity Projection](https://github.com/ErikEkstedt/VoiceActivityProjection) · [Full-Duplex-Bench](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.23159)

Use self-generated fixtures for repeatable degraded-state runs. For human tests capture separate participant and bot tracks only with consent. A listener-side audio tap can measure the audible tail after barge-in. An echo gate that mutes the mic trace during bot playback invalidates that measurement. Track human observations, hosted inference, local frame delivery and durable receipts separately. [Audio timing harness](https://github.com/jptaylor/pipecat-dev-tools) · [LiveKit turn handling](https://docs.livekit.io/agents/logic/turns/) · [Discord media protocol](https://docs.discord.com/developers/topics/voice-connections)

Review reconciliation specifically for original voice grant, historical supervisor, stored-attempt revalidation, audience checks and current release completion deduplication. Negative controls must reject stale actors/attempts without suppressing a new independent request.

## Plan review and decision boundaries

The reviewer found that an inactivity check in the native admission loop could be delayed until the separate hard call deadline. The AFK test now holds that loop, injects silence and voiced frames independently and checks cancellation, a fresh generation and the later written result. A repeated invitation issued before expiry must not act as a later human request. A connector or manager restart must restore durable discussion evidence without restoring permission to play old audio.

A queued team answer and an answer already playing require different decisions. Delay the queued answer while an eligible human holds the floor. Preserve ongoing playback for unaddressed chatter. Domain authority still requires an addressed, attributed utterance. Pair tests with zero misses on clean intentional interruptions; abstaining on all audio would fail.

The one-frame personal interruption budget above was a prospective target. A two-frame gate still accepted three of six offset 20 ms pops. Three frames rejected all six but added about 40 ms over the original gate, **missing the original budget**. Carry this explicitly into the human trial: compare promptness and accidental stops, and retain the prior 500 ms listener-side target. No timing-only threshold separated every acknowledgement from every short intentional stop in the local speech sweep. Do not classify “yes” as disposable without knowing whether the dispatcher asked for confirmation.

## Research trade-offs

|Method|Primary observation|Applicability to Swarm|
|---|---|---|
|Joint duplex modelling|Moshi models the two audio streams concurrently; its authors reported roughly 200 ms practical model latency.|Do not add model latency to a cross-run native median or call that listener latency. Measure floor change, recognition, durable admission, verified result and playback independently. [Moshi paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.00037)|
|Adaptive interruption|LiveKit documents detecting backchannels and optionally resuming falsely interrupted output.|A resumable verified result needs a trustworthy playback cursor and unchanged audience. Replaying the entire publication after an uncertain disconnect can duplicate speech; default to the canonical thread in that case. [Turn tuning](https://docs.livekit.io/agents/logic/turns/tuning/)|
|Multiparty addressing|Duplex-MPE pairs explicitly and implicitly addressed requests in three- and four-speaker situations.|The supported team contract requires an explicit address. Grade implicit group questions as expected abstentions. Add side talk, quoted names, two simultaneous addressed turns, quiet participants and a late worker publication. [Duplex-MPE](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.31948)|
|Two-channel projection and overlap benchmarks|Voice Activity Projection and Full-Duplex-Bench separate holding/yielding, feedback and simultaneous talk.|Use matched onset offsets, durations and speaker tracks. A generated PCM trial proves the code path and clock budget. It cannot establish whether human listeners judge the interruption natural. [VAP](https://github.com/ErikEkstedt/VoiceActivityProjection) · [Benchmark harness](https://github.com/DanielLin94144/Full-Duplex-Bench)|
