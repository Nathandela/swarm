TASK: the owner chose "Solid Wedge" from six candidates in docs/design/icon-candidates/. The
app's launcher icon is still the placeholder shell prompt -- a white chevron and a rounded
cursor rule -- so the gate that says "the shipped mark IS the chosen candidate" fails.

WHAT THE FOUR ROWS ASSERT, and why two of them are green before any artwork moves:

  TestLauncherForegroundIsTheChosenIconCandidate   RED. The geometry join. It parses
      solid-wedge.svg and ic_launcher_foreground.xml, evaluates both path dialects into
      absolute points, and compares. It fails at the structural step: the candidate is one
      stroke-only path plus one fill-only path, the placeholder is two stroked paths and no
      filled shape at all.

  TestLauncherForegroundSpendsOnlyTokenColours     RED. The colour join, SVG literal -> token
      -> resource. The placeholder is painted @color/swarm_text_primary (--p-ink, #FFF7F8F8);
      the candidate paints #FF53CE7C, which is --p-hero. Both paths are reported.

  TestLauncherAdaptiveIconDeclaresAllThreeLayers   GREEN, and honestly so. The two mipmap
      files already declare background, foreground and monochrome, and the background already
      resolves through design-tokens.tsv to --p-bg, which is the colour the candidate's ground
      rect paints. This row is a fence over a fact that already holds: replacing the artwork
      must not lose the Android 13 themed-icon layer. It is written now rather than after,
      because the value of a fence is that it is standing before the thing it guards moves.

  TestLauncherIconComparisonCanActuallyFail        GREEN. The negative control. Every other row
      is "these two files agree", which passes both when they agree and when the comparator
      cannot tell them apart. This drives the same comparator with seven perturbed geometries
      (a coordinate moved a tenth of a dp, the stroke back at 10, rounded caps, rounded joins,
      a wider cursor, a closed zigzag, a 96 canvas) and requires each to be rejected, plus two
      pairs of paths spelled differently in the two dialects that must compare EQUAL. It is
      perturbed IN MEMORY: the working tree is shared with other agents.
