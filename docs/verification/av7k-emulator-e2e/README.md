# av7k evidence: the camera pipeline decodes end to end (emulator, 2026-08-05)

Verdict: DECODED. The installed app, on an Android emulator whose virtual-scene wall carried
a clean QR poster (16 px/module, 4-module quiet zone, generated by
internal/remote/qrterm/poster_for_emulator_test.go), left the scan step and rendered the
destination-confirm screen showing the exact relay URL encoded in the poster
(decode-confirm-screen.png).

The HAL stream configuration (hal-stream-config.txt) shows ImageAnalysis granted exactly the
1280x720 that QrScanner.analysisResolution() requests.

What this rules out for the field failure: permission, use-case binding, the analyzer
thread, Y-plane extraction, ZXing on clean input, and the payload codec. What it cannot
rule out: the owner's symbol quality (terminal raster), exposure on a real glossy panel,
and the resolution a real Samsung grants -- those are the committee's remaining suspects,
carried by agents-tracker-av7k.

Method note: the poster lands on a wall the default camera position does not face; the
shipped macro walks to it:
  adb emu automation play "$ANDROID_HOME/emulator/resources/macros/Walk_to_image_room"

Anomaly recorded, not explained: ~6 s from first streamed frame to decode of a large clean
static symbol (low-fps virtual scene; do not over-read), and ~6 s of CameraX init retries
before open because this AVD has no front camera (CameraIdListIncorrectException; not an
app defect).
