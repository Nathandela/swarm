## Part 1 -- the wiring, over the source (Go)

`PhoneRuntime.phone()` answers `PhoneStartup.Unavailable` on every JVM run: the phone core is a
native library cross-compiled for Android ABIs, so `SettingsSurface.render` takes the Unavailable
branch, the settings panel is never drawn, `drawn` is never written, and no press can be driven as
far as a dialog -- let alone to a settle. A Robolectric test asserting "the first press did not
revoke" would pass against a surface with NO confirmation at all, because the press it drove did
nothing either way. That is the same line android/gate/d0b8_unpair_test.go draws over the same
function, and its header records the reasoning at length.

So which function the chip's listener reaches, which function reaches the verb, which view takes
the confirming tap, and what the dismiss does are fenced over the source.

## The two that were GREEN in this run, and why that is the honest result

`PairedMachineRowTest`'s other seven assertions read `label`, `sublabel` and `replaceLabel`, which
all exist. They are untouched by this change and stayed green, which is what a scaffolded red is
supposed to leave behind: the four new assertions failed alone.

PASSED here, over the three controls the surface declared at this commit -- the two push switches
and the replace chip. It is the anti-vacuity half of its file and it GAINS its subject with the
fix: once the confirming control is in `touchFilteredActions`, that sweep is what asserts the
overlay filter reaches the view an overlay attack would actually be mounted against. Recorded as
green rather than quietly omitted.
