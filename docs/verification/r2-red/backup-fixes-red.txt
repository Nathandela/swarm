This run happened to surface as a Go-level assertion panic rather than a raw
SIGBUS (this particular truncation offset left the OS's page-rounded mmap
tail zero-filled rather than genuinely unmapped, so bbolt read zeroed
"page 44" instead of faulting on unmapped memory outright) -- but the process
still dies: "[recovered, repanicked]" is testing.tRunner deliberately
re-panicking past its own recovery to crash the whole binary, exactly the
"operator's restore process dies mid-command" failure the finding reports.
The reviewer's own reproduction (truncating to exactly half of a 24576-byte
backup) got the harder SIGBUS instead; both are the same underlying defect
(meta.pgid exceeds the pages the truncated file's mmap actually covers) and
both are equally fatal to the calling process.
