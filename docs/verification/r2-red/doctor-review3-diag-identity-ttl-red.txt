Scope: playbook 4.1/6.5, hggx.3.2. internal/remote/relay/diag.go,
       internal/remote/relay/server.go (serverConn.diagExpiresAt).

Reviewer findings under test:
  1. LOW-MEDIUM: DiagnosticCapabilityTTL bounded only diag_open -- once
     sc.diagOpen was set, diag_append/diag_read/diag_status had no expiry for
     the rest of the connection. Probe: open a route, advance the clock 100x
     the TTL, diag_status still answered.
  2. LOW (design): signDiagCap's MAC covers only (context, nonce, issued_at)
     -- a pure unaudienced bearer token, replayable against the real relay
     under a throwaway identity by any endpoint the capability was ever shown
     to (typo'd URL, hijacked DNS).

diag_test.go changed FIRST, to the new contract:
  - MintDiagnosticCapability(secret, now, rid) -- an added identity-binding
    parameter, rid the caller's RoutingID(pub).
  - parseDiagCap returns (nonce, issuedAt, rid, err) -- one more value.
  - New tests: TestDiag_CapabilityBoundToADifferentIdentityIsRefused,
    TestDiag_RouteOpsAreRefusedOnceTheCapabilityTTLPasses,
    TestDiag_RouteExpiresRelativeToCapabilityIssuedAtNotOpenTime.
  - TestDiag_CapabilityIsSingleUse rewritten to replay from the SAME identity
    (a second connection under one routing id, per
    TestRelay_DuplicateConnectionResolved) so it cannot be conflated with the
    new identity-binding check.
None of this compiles against the current diag.go/server.go: MintDiagnosticCapability
and parseDiagCap still have their OLD two/three-value shapes, and sc.diagExpiresAt
does not exist yet.
