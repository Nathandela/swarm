(mobile/relay.go). Green afterwards, including the durable restart leg.

whole websocket (the websocket library's cancel-during-write contract), and the
reseed append then raced the connection the nudge had just killed:
  s10_resync_test.go:172: the first Resync: swarm/offline: relay: connection closed

FIX: the nudge is DEFERRED to run last in Resync, strictly after the reseed request
crossed; its worst case is one silent reconnect onto the rewound cursor. 6/6 green
under -race repetition afterwards.
