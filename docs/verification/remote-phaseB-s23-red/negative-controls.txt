== What these controls found in the fences themselves ==

Two of the seventeen mutations PASSED on the first run, and both were defects in this slice's own
gate rather than in the kit:

1. `elevation = 2f` inside an `apply {}` block -- which is how every view in this kit is
   configured -- was invisible to the PB-DS-5 fence, because the list held only `setElevation` and
   `.elevation` and the idiomatic Kotlin spelling has no receiver and no dot. The fence was blind
   to exactly the form the mistake would be written in.

2. A raw `12` as the FIRST argument of a multi-line `setPaddingRelative(...)` was invisible to the
   raw-dimension scan, because its argument list was bounded by `[^)]*`, which stops at the first
   close paren -- inside the `Kit.dimen(context, ...)` of the NEXT argument. The scan was
   inspecting a fragment of the call and reporting on the rest by accident.

Both are fixed, both now fire (recorded above), and both carry a probe inside the test so the
recognisers cannot quietly stop matching. The first attempt at control 1 was itself written as a
COMMENT and the gate correctly ignored it; that is recorded above rather than deleted, because a
fence a comment can trip is one the next thorough commenter turns into noise.
