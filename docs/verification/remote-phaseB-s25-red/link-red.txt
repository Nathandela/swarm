# S25 link section: failing-first run (GG-5), agents-tracker-ah2

The defect: `ClockBanner` and `StreamView`/`StreamBadge` were fully modelled, fully unit-tested,
reached by `FacadeBridge` -- and drawn by nothing. `FacadeBridge.clockBanner` and
`FacadeBridge.streamView` had no caller outside the adapter and both were ledgered in
android/unbound-verbs.tsv as models waiting for a screen.

ONE CORRECTION TO THE BRIEF, recorded because it changes what was fixed. The bead names three
models; `FacadeBridge.machineFreshness` is NOT one of them. It has a production caller at
PhoneSurface.kt:790, on the inbox status line beside `connectionBanner().text`, and reaches the
machines screen a second way through `MachinePane.presenceExplanation`. It is correspondingly
absent from the unbound ledger. The orphans were two.

Captured in two parts, because the two halves fail in different languages and only one of them
can fail without compiling.
