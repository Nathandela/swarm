# Second failing-first run: the three components row 11-13 actually need

The screen above was built on `settingsRow` for all three of its rows. That was wrong, and the
reason is worth keeping: the §4 rows this slice was briefed from were DUPLICATES of §3 rows 11, 12
and 13, which had specified the machines screen completely all along. Once the duplicates were
deleted (e8af092) and the numbered rows read, two of the three blocks turned out to be components
of their own -- row 11's shape is a leading mark, a name and a trailing mono identifier over a meta
line, which no existing factory has, and row 12 is the one bordered container in this app with no
fill. Row 13's Revoke is likewise the deny button's colours at the scope chip's METRICS, which
neither `ctaButton` nor `filterChip` renders.

So `machineRow`, `killSwitchPanel` and `denyChip` were added to `s23Inbox`, and their padding,
margins and internal gaps were joined to rows 11 and 12 through `s23DerivedSpacing` and
`s23DerivedEdge`. This is what those rows said before the files existed.

## What this run says that the first one could not

The four spacing faults are the interesting ones. `TestPBDS7_EveryDerivedSpacingIsTheRowsStep` and
`TestPBDS7_EveryPerEdgeSpacingIsTheRowsStep` read rows 11 and 12 out of the derivation table BEFORE
either component existed, resolved every cell they were asked for -- `padding space_12 x space_14`,
`top-line gap space_8`, `meta space_4 below`, `margin space_8 top / space_14 sides` -- and reported
only the absent files. No row reported "no such row exists" and none reported a cell it could not
parse, which is the half of a failing-first run that says the EXPECTATION is real: the numbers
these components are about to be built against were read out of the design, not written down twice.

The Kotlin lane is again absent for the reason the first run gives: `MachineRowTest`,
`KillSwitchPanelTest` and `DenyChipTest` fail to COMPILE before their subjects exist, and capturing
that needs `./gradlew`, which this agent does not run.
