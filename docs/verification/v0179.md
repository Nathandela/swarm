# v0.17.9 verification

Date: 2026-10-15. Branch `fix/v0179-integration`, from main `08ac3f50`. Evidence logs are kept in
the lab directory `swarm-lab/impl/v0179/` (`S1` to `S5`, `integration`). Root causes are in the lab
reports `R1-rotator-v0178.md` and `R2-board-staleness.md`.

## Scope

- **S1 (swarm-oj5), ADR-032 Amendment 2.**
  - Identity drift is reported only on a successful read whose identity differs. A failed read is
    logged and retried.
  - The hold is released when the identity matches again. A needs-login that drift set is
    restored; a login-expired one never is.
  - Input-lock refusals name the job that holds the lock and a remedy that actually unlocks.
  - Upgrade activation and rollback refuse a target card without `account_auth` while an account
    carries `auth_reason`.
- **S2 (swarm-8xz), ADR-032 Amendment 1.**
  - An automated CLI-refresh relaunch of an unbound discussion stays an owner launch, even with
    rotation on.
  - A relaunch that fails 3 times blocks the refresh durably and releases the lock. `swarm refresh`
    shows the reason.
- **S3 (swarm-e8s).** The board's elapsed column counts from the last group change.
- **S4 (swarm-kvj).** An out-of-order SubagentStop releases its child, unless it predates that
  child's incarnation.
- **S5 (swarm-ymm).** A failed status persist is logged and retried at most once per second until
  it lands.
- **PR #60.** Late startup rosters are applied, and journal pagination is linear.
