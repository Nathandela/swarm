# v0.17.10 verification

Date: 2026-10-15. Branch `fix/v0180-integration`, from main `d655b59c` (v0.17.9). Evidence logs
are in the lab directory `swarm-lab/impl/v0180/` (`T1` to `T4`, `integration`).

## Scope

- **T1 (swarm-ly0), ADR-032 Amendment 3.**
  - Managed Claude accepts any stable release from 2.1.288 and runs the owner's installed
    binary. Retained copies are no longer selected.
  - A bound resume never runs a CLI older than the one that last wrote the conversation
    (`cli-older-than-source`).
  - Rollback below worker capability 6 is refused while account state depends on a newer Claude.
  - The retirement sweep skips a generation whose files cannot be erased yet, and continues with
    the other accounts.
- **T2 (swarm-tr4), ADR-032 Amendment 4.**
  - A launch whose account was chosen by the selector falls back to the owner login when the
    account refuses without a code. This includes a managed check that fails while the launch is
    being composed.
  - The fallback is logged. Its notice shows as a board banner and in `swarm pilot`
    create/resume JSON.
  - Navigation is unchanged: the launch auto-attaches as before.
  - Launch-context failures, frozen bindings and `cli-older-than-source` still abort.
- **T3 (swarm-1im).** The daemon log records rotation toggles, account auth transitions with
  their cause, and changes in usage-fetch error class.
- **T4 (swarm-cm5).** The Codex "You're out of Codex messages" modal is read as needs-input. The
  title, the "Press a number to choose" footer and the numbered choice row must all be in the
  bottom region.

## Release gate and residual risks

- **Release gate.** The managed Claude paths assume that Claude 2.1.290 and later keep the 2.1.289
  `CLAUDE_CONFIG_DIR` file contracts. When they do not, the paths fail closed. Acceptance kit
  checks c1-c4 on the installed Claude must pass before this release is activated on a live
  daemon.
- Credential erasure for Claude generations last used by 2.1.290 or later stays refused until
  their inventory is qualified (Phase D).
- The fallback notice is not shown on the phone or by `swarm spawn`, `swarm handoff` or
  `swarm relogin`.
