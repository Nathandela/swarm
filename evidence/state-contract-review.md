Cleanup also runs before an overflow-driven compaction, with no terminal finish or message error ([processor.ts:609–695](https://github.com/anomalyco/opencode/blob/e3c4eb242585dcd77be1a867391b7c25fd47ce52/packages/opencode/src/session/processor.ts#L609-L695)).
